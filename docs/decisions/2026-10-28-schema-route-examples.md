- **SCHEMA's examples of a route made true (Book III,
  [#547](https://github.com/jackChallis/stoicheia/issues/547)).** The coordinator's rulings
  of 28 October 2026 on #547. This entry claims no approval of the owner's.

  The example of a `modified` route that `review/SCHEMA.md` gave, III.8, becomes false
  when #547's III.8 lands: its last step then goes by Euclid's two orders, and its route is
  `euclid`. The example is now III.27, whose reductio, where the angles at the
  circumference are obtuse, runs on the angles standing on the circumferences that remain,
  as review/III/27.toml records. The companion `Prop24.equalCircles` now stands beside
  III.24's `euclid` route, as review/III/24.toml records.

  The example of a `case` passage changes too, to one that fits the wording of the ruling
  on [#546](https://github.com/jackChallis/stoicheia/issues/546): III.11's A on GF
  produced beyond F, a case Heath says Euclid would have left to the reader (vol. 2,
  p. 26), as review/III/11.toml records under its `euclid` route. It takes the place of
  III.2's AB through the centre. II.14's example stands.

  Three examples in brackets change, and nothing else: no rule of the schema changes, and
  none is loosened. This entry approves the change to `review/SCHEMA.md`, a protected
  path, in those three examples alone.
