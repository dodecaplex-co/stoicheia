- **Two rulings on the Jev calibration set, made before any request of it is sent (#36).**
  [The bar's entry](2026-10-25-jev-calibration-bar.md) of 25 October 2026 fixes the
  calibration set by rule, and stops the work for a ruling when the set cannot be made as
  it says. Two such questions arose while the generator was reviewed, before any request
  of the new calibration, or of the audit it gates, had been sent, and on 26 October 2026
  the coordinator ruled on both. These are the coordinator's rulings, not the owner's;
  the coordinator's record of them is
  [#36](https://github.com/jackChallis/stoicheia/issues/36). This entry
  states them beside the bar's entry, which stands as it was written; where the two
  differ, this entry governs the calibration. Nothing else changes: the questions, their
  clean and defect answers, the order, the run, the bar's four conditions and what
  clearing or missing the bar means are as the bar's entry gives them.

  **P12's fourth kind of fault is dropped.** The bar's entry gives P12 four kinds of
  fault, the fourth "an existing hypothesis mentioned in a step that does no work",
  expecting "an artificial hypothesis use". P12's own option for that answer reads "a
  hypothesis is mentioned only so that it looks used, and does no substantive work". The
  independent review of the generator found that the two plants the rule gives this kind,
  on I.40 and III.31, mention a hypothesis that does substantive work elsewhere in its
  proof, so that neither is the fault the answer names. The kind, as the bar's entry words
  it, asks only that the step do no work, not that the hypothesis it mentions do no other
  work, so its plants need not be that fault, and these two are not. Left in, the kind
  would score a right answer, "all do work", as a missed fault. The
  coordinator's ruling: "The fourth kind is dropped, and the ten plants take kinds 1 to 3
  in turn, by the same rule and kind tests." P12's kinds are now three:

  - a call of an unrelated proposition whose result is not used (expects "a dummy
    citation");
  - a call whose result is bound while the step it is for is done another way (expects "a
    dummy citation"; its test: the unit's body calls a proposition);
  - a hypothesis added and used only in a binding nothing uses (expects "an artificial
    hypothesis use").

  The ten plants take them in turn as the bar's entry says, counting round three kinds
  instead of four, and a plant whose kind due does not apply takes the next kind that
  does.

  **P13's list for Book III is restated.** The bar's entry fixes the lists "at the records
  of main on the day of this entry". Since then the theorems on the angle of contact have
  moved from Staging into III.18's Lean file, and III.18's record, read in full again,
  gives its representations item as `pass`, where it gave `n/a` (commits `ccf50c11f` and
  `f94a335b8`, #130), so that III.18 is eligible for P13 and the hash rule gives Book III
  another list. The coordinator's ruling: "Book III's
  list is restated by the same hash rule at a named commit of main: III.18 becomes a
  control, and III.29 leaves the set." The named commit is
  `110a01809ed48fc49871137d9a0fb968cad92d11`, the head of main when the ruling was made.
  It is named because at it III.18's record carries the change that made it eligible, and
  the records give, by the bar's rule, every other list exactly as the bar's entry does:
  P13's lists for Books I and II, and every list of P11, P12, P14 and P15. At that commit
  P13's list for Book III is (controls; then plants):

  | Question | Book III |
  |---|---|
  | P13 | III.18, III.33, III.24, III.26; then III.37, III.28, III.27, III.36 |

  III.37, a control in the bar's entry, is now a plant, and III.29 leaves the set. The
  plants of Book III take P13's kinds in turn, as the bar's entry says.

  The bar's check of the lists now reads against the bar's lists, with P13's list for
  Book III as this entry restates it: if the records at the calibration's commit give,
  for any question and book, other lists, nothing is sent, and the work stops for a
