  owner chose, of the options put to them, "Three cold reviewers" (each scoring every book
  at least 95 on the rubric, on the release candidate, as the independent audit), and then,
  when a probe found the third reader's review unreliable (an answer written without
  re-reading, with citations that do not exist), said: "Just keep to Claude and Astra. It's
  fine. Better than an unreliable reader like Qwen or Argon." Those words are the owner's;
  the coordinator's record of them is
  [#703](https://github.com/dodecaplex-co/stoicheia/issues/703).

  copy withholds the files that show scores (the rubric's step 1), so that each reviewer
