- **III.21's route becomes `euclid`, with Simson's completion as a declared case (Book III,
  [#704](https://github.com/dodecaplex-co/stoicheia/issues/704)).**
  This is the owner's ruling. On 29 October 2026 the owner chose, of the options put to
  them, "euclid (Recommended)"; the coordinator's orders of 29 October 2026, 20:20, record
  it. The other option was `modified`, with the further cases as a `different` passage.

  It follows the coordinator's ruling of 28 October 2026
  ([2026-10-28-route-labels-iii20-iii21.md](2026-10-28-route-labels-iii20-iii21.md)),
  which made III.21 `modified` because the doubling by III.20 for the semicircle and the
  less segment rested on our angle at the centre and not on Euclid's. The proof no longer
  uses that angle there, so that ground is gone; the entry of 28 October stays as it
  stands, as the record of the proof it judged.

  The rule applied is the one [review/SCHEMA.md](../../review/SCHEMA.md) gives under
  `[route]`: helpers, groupings and cases Euclid leaves to the reader do not change the
  route, and are declared as passages. Heath says Euclid's proof reaches only the segment
  greater than a semicircle (vol. 2, p. 49). The Lean carries his two steps there, on the
  angle BFD, by III.20's companion `Prop20.special`. It takes the semicircle and the less
  segment by Robert Simson's completion, which Heath gives (vol. 2, p. 50): AF is drawn
  through the centre to C, and Euclid's case, written for any base as the file's helper
  `Prop21.greater`, is taken twice. Record 21
  declares that completion as a `case` passage under "Therefore etc.", as record 35
  declares Simson's intermediate cases of III.35 under a `euclid` route.

  No statement changes, nor any porism, companion, definition, axiom, lock or `--` line.
