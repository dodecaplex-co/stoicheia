- **III.20's route stays `euclid`, with its further case declared, and III.21's becomes
  `modified` (Book III, [#546](https://github.com/jackChallis/stoicheia/issues/546)).**
  This is the coordinator's ruling of 28 October 2026, recorded on #546; it claims no
  approval of the owner's.

  The rule applied is the one [review/SCHEMA.md](../../review/SCHEMA.md) gives under
  `[route]`. A case that lies beyond Euclid's statement, which the Lean takes as well, does
  not change the route: it is declared as a passage. A real case within his statement,
  argued another way, does: the passage is `different`, and the route is `modified`.

  III.20 is the first kind. Euclid's angles at the centre are less than two right angles,
  so where the angle BAC is right or obtuse his statement has no angle at the centre on the
  circumference BC (Heath, vol. 2, p. 48). Our angle at the centre, which may be two right
  angles or more, lets the Lean take that case as well, by his first figure, and record 20
  declares it as a `case` passage under "Therefore etc.". Every step within his statement
  is his, so the route stays `euclid`.

  III.21 is the second kind. The semicircle and the less segment are cases of his
  statement, though Heath says his proof reaches only a segment greater than a semicircle
  (vol. 2, p. 49). There the Lean's first step, the doubling by III.20, rests on our angle
  at the centre and not on Euclid's, so record 21 marks it `different`, and the route is
  `modified`, with one step changed. Simson's completion is still not used.

  No statement changes, nor any porism, companion, definition, axiom, lock or `--` line;
  two remarks in `Elements/BookIII/Prop21.lean`, comments only, follow the records.
