- **III.27 and III.29 state Euclid's congruence reading, and their measure forms become
  companions (Book III, [#474](https://github.com/dodecaplex-co/stoicheia/issues/474)).**
  The owner was asked, on 28 October 2026: "III.27 and III.29: ... ER proposes swapping
  them, so the main theorem is the congruence reading and the measure version becomes the
  companion. ... Swap the statements?" On 28 October 2026 the owner chose, of the options
  put to them, "Yes, swap". Issue #474 holds the coordinator's record of the question and
  the choice. The question and the choice are the owner's part in this entry, and it
  claims no other approval of theirs; the rest is the coordinator's. The coordinator's
  ruling takes the choice so: `Prop27` and `Prop29` state Euclid's congruence reading, and
  the measure forms become companions under their own names; nothing is narrowed, both
  forms stay proved, and later citations move to the companions.

  Here III.27's main statement, `Prop27` in `Elements/BookIII/Prop27.lean`, is approved in
  its new form: the angles stand on circumferences BC, EF that coincide when one is placed
  on the other (`Arc.Congruent`), named by Q across BC from A and R across EF from D, the
  form `Prop27.of_congruent` stated until now.
  Here III.29's main statement, `Prop29` in `Elements/BookIII/Prop29.lean`, is approved in
  its new form: the equal straight lines subtend circumferences BGC, EHF that coincide, the
  form `Prop29.of_congruent` stated until now.

  ```lean
  theorem Prop27 {A B C D E F G H Q R : Point} {ABC DEF : Circle}
      (hcircles : EqualCircles ABC DEF) (hG : Centre G ABC) (hH : Centre H DEF)
      (hA : OnCircle A ABC) (hB : OnCircle B ABC) (hC : OnCircle C ABC)
      (hD : OnCircle D DEF) (hE : OnCircle E DEF) (hF : OnCircle F DEF)
      (hAQ : OppositeSidesOf A Q B C) (hDR : OppositeSidesOf D R E F)
      (hcong : Arc.Congruent ⟨ABC, B, C, Q⟩ ⟨DEF, E, F, R⟩) :
      ∠ B A C = ∠ E D F ∧
        (SameSideOf G A B C → SameSideOf H D E F → ∠ B G C = ∠ E H F)

  theorem Prop29 {B C E F G H : Point} {ABC DEF : Circle}
      (hcircles : EqualCircles ABC DEF)
      (hB : OnCircle B ABC) (hC : OnCircle C ABC) (hE : OnCircle E DEF) (hF : OnCircle F DEF)
      (hG : ¬ Collinear B C G) (hH : ¬ Collinear E F H)
      (hcong : Arc.Congruent ⟨ABC, B, C, G⟩ ⟨DEF, E, F, H⟩) :
      ‖B C‖ = ‖E F‖
  ```

  III.27's new companion, `Prop27.of_measure` ("III.27 of_measure"), is approved with the
  statement `Prop27` had until now: the angles BAC, EDF are given by their triangles, and
  the circumferences on which they stand are equal in measure (`standing`).
  III.29's new companion, `Prop29.of_measure` ("III.29 of_measure"), is approved with the
  statement `Prop29` had until now: the circumferences BGC, EHF are equal in measure
  (`circumference`).

  ```lean
  theorem Prop27.of_measure {A B C D E F G H : Point} {ABC DEF : Circle}
      (hcircles : EqualCircles ABC DEF)
      (hG : Centre G ABC) (hH : Centre H DEF) (hA : OnCircle A ABC) (hB : OnCircle B ABC)
      (hC : OnCircle C ABC) (hD : OnCircle D DEF) (hE : OnCircle E DEF)
      (hF : OnCircle F DEF) (hBAC : Triangle B A C) (hEDF : Triangle E D F)
      (heq : standing ABC B A C = standing DEF E D F) :
      ∠ B A C = ∠ E D F ∧
        (SameSideOf G A B C → SameSideOf H D E F → ∠ B G C = ∠ E H F)

  theorem Prop29.of_measure {B C E F G H : Point} {ABC DEF : Circle}
      (hcircles : EqualCircles ABC DEF)
      (hB : OnCircle B ABC) (hC : OnCircle C ABC) (hE : OnCircle E DEF) (hF : OnCircle F DEF)
      (hG : ¬ Collinear B C G) (hH : ¬ Collinear E F H)
      (heq : circumference ABC B G C = circumference DEF E H F) :
      ‖B C‖ = ‖E F‖
  ```

  How the four are proved. `Prop27` carries Euclid's argument under Heath's sentences, which
  stay where they were: its first step makes the coinciding circumferences equal in
  measure, by III.24's companion `Prop24.equalMeasure`, where Heath gives no reference, and
  the reductio and the halving in each case follow as before. Its record declares that step
  as a passage, and its certificate gains the citation "III.24 equalMeasure". The companion
  `Prop27.of_measure` repeats the same argument from the given measures, with remarks of
  ours in place of Heath's sentences. It is not proved from `Prop27`, which would need
  circumferences equal in measure to coincide: `Arc.congruent_iff` says so only after
  III.29, and rests on it, so that proof would go round in a circle. Nor is it proved by the
  short derivation from the measure alone that `Audit/Overlaps.lean` keeps, which is not
  Euclid's route. `Prop29` takes the proof `Prop29.of_congruent` had, now citing `Prop27`,
  with Heath's sentences of III.29 moved onto it, their words unchanged; `Prop29.of_measure`
  takes the proof `Prop29` had, citing `Prop27.of_measure`. `Arc.measure_eq_iff`, in
  `Elements/BookIII/Arcs.lean`, now cites `Prop29.of_measure`. Neither main proof is a
  single call of a helper or a companion, and no helper carries a whole conclusion.

  The companions list, `Elements/Companions.lean`, changes with this entry's approval: in
  place of its entries "III.27 of_congruent" and "III.29 of_congruent" it now lists
  "III.27 of_measure" and "III.29 of_measure", at the same places, and its imports stay as
  they are.
  The audit file `Audit/Overlaps.lean` changes with this entry's approval: the `example`
  under `Audit.III_27_any` gives back the type of `Prop27.of_measure`, since `Prop27` names
  no measure now, and the file's prose about III.27 says so; `III_27_any` itself and its
  pinned axioms do not change.

  No check, test, expected value, lock or schema is weakened, and no axiom or rule is added.
  No porism or definition changes, and no `--` line's words. `Prop27.of_congruent` and
  `Prop29.of_congruent` came after the book-III release of 17 October, so no release
  declares them, and they go without deprecated aliases (FORMALIZATION, section 2, "Names,
  and how they change"); the next release's notes are to list the new statements of
  `Prop27` and `Prop29`, with their old forms now at `Prop27.of_measure` and
  `Prop29.of_measure`.

  This entry supersedes the naming in
  [the entry on the congruent companions](2026-10-28-congruent-companions.md), of the same
  day, without rewriting it: the statements it approved as `Prop27.of_congruent` and
  `Prop29.of_congruent` are now `Prop27` and `Prop29`, and the statements it left as
  `Prop27` and `Prop29` are now `Prop27.of_measure` and `Prop29.of_measure`. What it says of
  "in equal circles" now reads the other way round for III.29: the hypothesis is redundant
  in `Prop29`, whose congruence already gives the equal circles and the equal straight
  lines, and does real work in `Prop29.of_measure`. Both records keep the tags
  `angle-measure` and `redundant-hypothesis`, and wait for an independent reader's stamp.
