- **A reductio's figure may draw the impossible supposition, as Heath's does (#220).** The
  Jev audit's adjudications split over three figures that draw a reductio's impossible
  supposition: for I.14, whose E stands off CB produced while the construction puts E on it,
  Claude agreed and Codex judged it defective; for I.6 and I.26, whose DB and whose BG and
  BH are drawn shorter than the AC, DE and EF they are cut off equal to, the other way
  round. In #220 a Codex reader judged I.14's figure defective and a Claude reader judged
  I.26's right. On 21 October 2026 the owner chose, of two conventions put to them, "Follow
  Heath, disclosed": a reductio's figure may draw the impossible supposition as Heath's
  does, provided the note says so. The other option, redrawing to the Lean, was declined.
  I.14's and I.26's figures stand, and [STYLE](../STYLE.md), section 7, says so under
  "Checked as geometry".
