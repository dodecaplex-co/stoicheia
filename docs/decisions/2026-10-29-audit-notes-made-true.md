- **Audit's notes made true: Circle 2 and Circle 3, and III.30's near miss (Audit,
  [#456](https://github.com/dodecaplex-co/stoicheia/issues/456),
  [#463](https://github.com/dodecaplex-co/stoicheia/issues/463)).** The coordinator's
  orders of 29 October 2026 put two stale notes in the Audit library among the release's
  small fixes. The note at the head of `Audit/Centre.lean` said that Circle 2 and Circle 3
  were theorems since 24 October 2026, but Circle 2 was proved on 19 October; it now says
  where both are proved, after III.2 in `Elements/BookIII/Prop02.lean` from its corollary
  `Prop2.within`, rather than when. Two notes in `Audit/Crossing.lean` gave both the same
  date, and now give each its own, 19 and 24 October 2026. The note over `III_30_measure`
  in `Audit/Overlaps.lean` said in the present tense that III.30's statement keeps the
  measure when arcs get a meaning of their own; that happened at stage 3 of brief 0006,
  and the overlap catalogue already says so in the past tense, as the note now does. Only
  comments change: no statement, proof, pin or axiom list moves.

  This entry approves the change to `Audit/Centre.lean`: its note on Circle 2 and Circle
  3 says where they are proved.

  This entry approves the change to `Audit/Crossing.lean`: its two notes date Circle 2 to
  19 October 2026.

  This entry approves the change to `Audit/Overlaps.lean`: the note over `III_30_measure`
  in the past tense.
