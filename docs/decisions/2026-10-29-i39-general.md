- **I.39's vertices may be one point: the companion `Prop39.general` (Book I,
  [#548](https://github.com/dodecaplex-co/stoicheia/issues/548)).** On 29 October 2026 the
  owner chose, of the options put to them, "Approve as drafted (Recommended)", for the
  statement below (card #548 is the coordinator's record of it). The rest of this entry
  describes the change made under that approval, and claims no further approval of the
  owner's.

  What it answers. `Prop39` assumes `hAD : A ≠ D`, while I.39's page said "The whole
  enunciation", and its review record defended the hypothesis as saying that the two
  triangles are two. The stand-in review found the scope claim resting on that reading,
  and showed that the case of one vertex follows from I.31. Two ways were put: a general
  companion, with that case by I.31; or a ruling, recorded on the page, that Euclid's equal
  triangles are two triangles. The first is taken, and no such ruling is made.

  This entry approves the statement of `Prop39.general`, the companion "I.39 general", in
  `Elements/BookI/Prop39.lean`, exactly as below, its docstring included:

  ```lean
  /-- **I.39, general** (companion of I.39). Equal triangles ABC, DBC on the same base BC of
  the straight line L, and on the same side of it, are in the same parallels: A and D lie on
  one straight line parallel to L. The vertices may be one point, and then the parallel is the
  one through it (I.31). -/
  theorem Prop39.general {A B C D : Point} {L : Line} (hABC : Triangle A B C)
      (hDBC : Triangle D B C) (hB : OnLine B L) (hC : OnLine C L)
      (heq : △ A B C = △ D B C) (hside : SameSide A D L) :
      ∃ M : Line, Parallel M L ∧ OnLine A M ∧ OnLine D M
  ```

  It reads "in the same parallels" as I.40's companion `Prop40.general` does: one straight
  line parallel to the base's straight line L holds both vertices. Its proof follows the
  route the card declares, and cites nothing after I.39. When A and D are two points,
  `SameSideOf.of_line` reads the same side of L as the same side of BC, `Prop39` gives AD
  parallel to BC, and the straight line through A and D (Postulate 1) is the parallel
  asked for. When they are one point, the parallel to L through it is the one I.31 draws
  (`Prop31.line`). It adds no rule, axiom or helper, and I.40 is not cited, since it comes
  later.

  This entry approves the change to `Elements/Companions.lean`, the companions list, which
  the integrator owns: it gains the entry "I.39 general", ahead of "I.40 general", and the
  import `Elements.BookI.Prop39` that the entry's name needs.

  What stays. `Prop39`'s statement and proof stand as they were, every `--` line with
  them, and no other statement, porism, definition, axiom or lock changes. I.39's record
  keeps its scope full, now by `Prop39` and `Prop39.general` together: it lists the
  companion, its statement note says which theorem covers which case, and its opening
  comment no longer argues that `hAD` reads "two triangles". The mishnah says in two
  sentences that the companion lets the vertices be one point, with the parallel through
  it by I.31. The record waits for an independent reader's stamp.
