- **Two corrections to the entry on the scan's token boundaries, and FORMALIZATION's
  sentence on the refused `#` commands
  ([#321](https://github.com/jackChallis/stoicheia/issues/321)).** The review of #234's
  last fix round, TT-234-cr4, found four points of wording. Three are in the entry of 22
  October,
  [the source scan ends a name as Lean 4.35 does](2026-10-22-scan-token-boundaries.md),
  which, being history, is not edited; this entry corrects it instead.

  Its opening paragraph gives three ways in which closed-up text hid a command from the
  scan, and the second, Lean's own tokens that begin with `#`, has four examples:
  `#check1set_option…`, `#whereaxiom…`, and the forms of `#time` and `#with_exporting`.
  The change that entry records closes only the last two. The first two stay open, as its
  paragraph on what stays open says, and
  [#320](https://github.com/jackChallis/stoicheia/issues/320) holds them.

  Where it reports the coordinator's second ruling, "the other two kept" are the refusal
  of a token the file declares, widened to every character a name may hold, and the
  refusal of `#guard`.

  The third point was a ragged wrap in that entry's paragraph on what stays open, beside
  its links to #317 and #320. It changes no meaning, and the wrap stays as it is.

  The fourth is in [FORMALIZATION](../FORMALIZATION.md), section 7, which said that
  "those the list above names" are refused whether closed up or not. The list names
  `#guard_msgs` as well, which is refused only in some forms, so the sentence now says
  "those the list above refuses in every form", before the six commands it lists.

  The coordinator filed the four points as #321, and ordered them done in a docs train on
  22 October. This entry claims no approval of the owner.
