- **The certificate's legend says what its C.N. column counts (checks,
  [#295](https://github.com/jackChallis/stoicheia/issues/295)).** The coordinator's orders
  of 26 October 2026 assign this change, from a cold review of main at cfab642 (22 October),
  which found that the column counts citations of the named Common Notion lemmas, not their
  logical use, and that the legend did not say so. `Certificate/Facts.lean` lists a Common
  Notion beneath a proof when the walk `allNotions` meets one of the lemmas in
  `commonNotions` (`CommonNotion1` to `CommonNotion5`, with `CommonNotion4.angle` and
  `CommonNotion4.triangle`), or, for Common Notion 4, a value naming the restricted
  calculus's coincidence step. Reasoning with equals by Lean's own logic (`rw`, `Eq.trans`)
  names none of them, so III.5's proof, which joins two equalities of lengths by
  `Eq.trans`, shows only Common Notion 5. The legend now says so in two sentences, after the
  sentence on the walk. No code path, input, check, count or other output changes; the
  regenerated `CERTIFICATE.md` differs from main's in that paragraph alone.

  This entry approves the change to `scripts/render.py`, where two sentences are added to
  the string literals of `certificate_intro` and nothing else changes.
