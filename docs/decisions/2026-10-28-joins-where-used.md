- **A join under a sentence of Euclid's is written in the Lean only where the formal proof
  uses the line it draws (proofs, part of
  [#482](https://github.com/jackChallis/stoicheia/issues/482)).** The coordinator, 28
  October 2026, 18:58, replacing the Tw2 ruling
  ([#32](https://github.com/jackChallis/stoicheia/issues/32);
  [the decisions page](../briefs/0002-decisions.md), "The joins by Postulate 1 stay
  wherever Euclid writes them", and its restatement in "The citation sweep" of 21
  October 2026): "A join under a sentence of Euclid's is written in the Lean only where
  the formal proof uses the line it draws. Heath's sentence stays as its `--` line in
  every case, so the text is unchanged, and the record says the join is drawn in the
  text and not needed by the formal proof." The ruling follows the owner's guidance of 22 October:
  "take out what Euclid does not use, disclose what a representation needs". The
  measurement of 28 October found idle joins deducted with Claude and Argon, and #482's
  removal of them earned 0.75 on Book I with Claude.

  So the joins of I.5, I.11, I.16, I.20, I.22, I.26, I.39 and I.40, which no later step
  of those proofs uses, are gone from the Lean: a straight line between two points is
  named by its ends, as a side of the triangle that uses it, and Postulate 1 is not
  called for it. Each of those passages now says, in its record's note, that the join is
  drawn in the text and is not needed by the formal proof. Where a later step does use
  the line a join draws, the join stays and cites Postulate 1. Tw2's entry stays on the
  page as the record of what was decided on its day; this entry replaces its ruling, and
  the sweep's declared hits no longer keep an idle join on its ground.
