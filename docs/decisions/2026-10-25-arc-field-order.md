- **The arc keeps its field order: brief 0010's question 3 answered (notation, #165).** On
  25 October 2026, at about 22:45 Pacific, the owner answered "yes to all three." to three
  recommendations the coordinator had put to them. Those words are the owner's. One of the
  three, recorded by the coordinator on
  [#165](https://github.com/jackChallis/stoicheia/issues/165), is the one this entry
  carries out: to answer question 3 of [brief 0010](../briefs/0010-notation.md), the arc's
  field order, by keeping the order the fields have in `Elements`. So `Arc`, in
  `Elements/Foundations/DefinitionsIII.lean`, keeps its fields `circle`, `B`, `C`, `K`, in
  that order, and that is question 3's answer.

  Nothing moves with it. No field changes place, and no statement changes: the six
  statements that build an arc by position, those of III.24, III.26 and III.28 and of the
  companions "III.25 complete", "III.25 kind" and "III.30 halves", keep the terms Lean
  records for them, so no fingerprint, `lean` stamp or lock entry changes. Heath's order
  for the fields (`circle`, `B`, `K`, `C`) is not taken. The recommendation names the field
  order alone, and this answer adds no function: a constructor in Heath's order,
  `Arc.ofHeath`, which the brief's section 3.3 says may be added unused before the release
  or after it, is no part of it, and a statement written with one would need an approval of
  its own. A statement writes Heath's arc BKC as `⟨α, B, C, K⟩`: the circle, then the two
  ends, then the point that names the side, the roles `Arc`'s docstring gives its fields.

  Question 3 asked for the choice before the consolidation release, since afterwards the
  fields could not move without silently changing the arcs written with them; it is made
  before that release. Brief 0012's question 8, which waited on it, now has its answer's
  premise: an arc in `Polygon.arcs_measure` is written in the order the fields have. Brief
  0010 is frozen, so it gains two dated notes pointing here, one after the note of 25
  October in its section 3.3 and one under question 3 in its section 8; nothing else in it
  changes.

  [The entry of 21 October](2026-10-21-briefs-0010-and-0012.md) said that an answer to
  question 3 would be recorded in an entry of its own; this is that entry.
