**"By way of practice."** Proclus carries the proposition "by way of practice" over to the trapezium, "a quadrilateral with only one pair of opposite sides parallel", as Heath explains it (vol. 1, p. 338). Heath gives one of his results in these words: "*If a triangle be formed by joining the middle point of either of the non-parallel sides to the extremities of the opposite side, the area of the trapezium is always double of that of the triangle*" (vol. 1, p. 338).

**The middle point.** In Proclus' figure E is the middle point of DC, and BE is produced to meet AD produced. By I.26 the two triangles at E are equal; add ABED to each, and the trapezium is equal to the triangle that the produced straight lines make on AB, which I.38 makes double of AEB (Heath, vol. 1, p. 339). Heath puts Proclus' results in one: the trapezium is greater than, equal to or less than double the triangle, as the middle point is taken on the greater parallel side, on a side that is not parallel, or on the lesser. The end of Proclus' note is lost, as Heath remarks under I.43.

**Where E may lie.** Our figure is Euclid's, with E on AD produced beyond D, but the statement lets E fall anywhere on the straight line AD, at A or at D among them. One argument serves for all, since our I.37 takes its parallels as whole straight lines and lets its two triangles share their vertex. I.34 then makes the parallelogram double of ABC, and so of EBC.

**What Lean made us say.** Joining AC asks nothing of Lean: `quadArea` already measures ABCD by its diameter AC, as the triangles ABC and ACD. The proof uses Postulate 1 to name the straight line BC, as I.37 wants it. E lies off BC, since a parallel to BC never meets it (`Parallel.not_onLine`), so EBC is a triangle. `Prop34.bisect` gives I.34's bisection as a double. The certificate cites I.34 and I.37, Euclid's brackets, and Postulate 5 comes in through I.29, on which both rest.

**The modern way.** Let b = C − B; then D − A = b, and E = A + t·b for some real number t. The parallelogram has area |b × (A − B)|, while EBC has area ½|b × (E − B)| = ½|b × (A − B)|, since b × b = 0.
