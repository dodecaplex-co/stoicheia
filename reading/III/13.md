- Jacques Hadamard revised his school geometry twelve times, and its book on the circle walks much of Book III's road. In Mark Saul's translation, [*Lessons in Geometry, I: Plane Geometry*](https://doi.org/10.1090/mbk/057) (2008), the Complements to Book III follow touching circles to circles touching three given circles: ch. VI, "The Problem of Tangent Circles" (p. 231), with Note C (pp. 297–302).
- Both arguments can be followed in colour, straight line by straight line, in Oliver Byrne's proof, rebuilt for the web by Nicholas Rougeux as [*Byrne's Euclid*](https://www.c82.net/euclid/) (2018, free online) (also on [III.5](05.md)).
