- For the Greek tables that turn circumferences into straight lines, Eli Maor's [*Trigonometric Delights*](https://press.princeton.edu/books/paperback/9780691202198/trigonometric-delights) (1998; paperback 2020), ch. 2, explains the geometry behind them: III.28 and III.29 let one table pair each circumference with its straight line in a circle of fixed size.
