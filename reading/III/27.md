- The motions behind the modern way, which carry a circumference onto any equal circumference, have a chapter of their own in Jacques Hadamard's [*Lessons in Geometry, I: Plane Geometry*](https://doi.org/10.1090/mbk/057) (2008): Book II, ch. VI, "On the Motion of Figures" (p. 99) (also on [III.13](13.md)).
- Felix Klein's Erlangen Programme of 1872, a short manifesto, translated by M. W. Haskell as ["A comparative review of recent researches in geometry"](https://arxiv.org/abs/0807.3161) (1893, free online), characterises a geometry by the transformations it allows, Euclid's by the rigid motions: a setting for the modern way.
