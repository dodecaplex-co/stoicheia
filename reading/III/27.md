- For the motions behind the modern way, which carry an arc onto any equal arc, Jacques Hadamard's [*Lessons in Geometry, I: Plane Geometry*](https://doi.org/10.1090/mbk/057) (2008), Book II, ch. VI, "On the Motion of Figures" (p. 99) (also on [III.13](13.md)).
- For the rigid motions of the modern way, Felix Klein's ["A comparative review of recent researches in geometry"](https://arxiv.org/abs/0807.3161) (1893, free online), his Erlangen Programme of 1872, in which a geometry is known by the motions it allows.
