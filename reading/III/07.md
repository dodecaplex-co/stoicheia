- For the gap De Morgan found here, "There is an unproved assumption", in his own words, with the remedy he proposes, Augustus De Morgan's ["Short Supplementary Remarks on the First Six Books of Euclid's Elements"](https://archive.org/details/sim_british-almanac-companion_1849) (1849, free online), the remarks on Book III (pp. 9–11), sixteen brisk pages of advice to teachers from which Heath draws his reports of De Morgan.
- For the crossbar theorem that closes De Morgan's gap, Marvin Jay Greenberg's [*Euclidean and Non-Euclidean Geometries*](https://store.macmillanlearning.com/us/product/Euclidean-and-Non-Euclidean-Geometries/p/0716799480) (2008), ch. 3, "Hilbert's Axioms", the chapter met under I.10, where betweenness, Pasch's axiom and the crossbar theorem are worked through.
