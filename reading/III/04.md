- For the proof by contradiction that rules out a common middle point, Daniel J. Velleman's [*How to Prove It*](https://doi.org/10.1017/9781108539890) (2019), §3.2, "Proofs Involving Negations and Conditionals", a friendly guide to assuming what a theorem denies and finding the impossibility.
- For the dot products in the modern way, Grant Sanderson's [*Essence of linear algebra*](https://www.3blue1brown.com/topics/linear-algebra) (2016, free online), ch. 9, "Dot products and duality", an animated explanation of how dot products express perpendicularity.
