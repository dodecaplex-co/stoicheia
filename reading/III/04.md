- What III.4 denies is that AC and BD bisect one another both at once, for either may bisect the other alone, and Euclid proves the denial by supposing both bisections. Daniel J. Velleman's [*How to Prove It*](https://doi.org/10.1017/9781108539890) (2019), a patient introduction to the logic under every proof, shows in §3.2, "Proofs Involving Negations and Conditionals", how such a denial is proved by supposing what it denies.
