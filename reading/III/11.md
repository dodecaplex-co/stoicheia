- For the reading of Definition III.3 that our `TouchesInternally` makes exact, that touching circles "do not cut at all", every point of the one within the other but the point of contact, Isaac Todhunter's [*The Elements of Euclid*](https://archive.org/details/todhuntereuclid00todhrich) (1872, free online), his note on the third definition (p. 271), in the notes met under III.3.
- For "the contact of circles is in a state of confusion in Euclid", and the "positive assumption" that a circle touching within lies entirely within the other, the assumption our definition carries, Augustus De Morgan's ["Short Supplementary Remarks on the First Six Books of Euclid's Elements"](https://archive.org/details/sim_british-almanac-companion_1849) (1849, free online), the remarks on III.11–13 (pp. 10–11), met under III.7.
