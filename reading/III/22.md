- For where the opposite-angle test leads, Kiran S. Kedlaya's [*Geometry Unbound*](https://kskedlaya.org/geometryunbound/) (2006, free online), ch. 8, "Quadrilaterals", from cyclic quadrilaterals on to Ptolemy's theorem and Brahmagupta's formula.
- For Ptolemy's theorem on the quadrilateral in a circle, put to work on his table of chords, G. J. Toomer's translation of [*Ptolemy's Almagest*](https://press.princeton.edu/isbn/9780691002606) (1998), a careful modern translation, for the curious.
