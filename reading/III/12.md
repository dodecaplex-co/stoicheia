- For Heron, whose addition became III.12, Thomas L. Heath's [*A History of Greek Mathematics*, vol. II](https://archive.org/details/cu31924008704227) (1921, free online) gives him a chapter of his own.
- For the editors who added to the *Elements*, Benjamin Wardhaugh's [*Encounters with Euclid: How an Ancient Greek Geometry Text Shaped the World*](https://press.princeton.edu/books/hardcover/9780691211695/encounters-with-euclid) (2021) follows the people who copied, taught and reshaped the book.
