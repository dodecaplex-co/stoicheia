- For the locus of the modern way, that triangles "on the same base and on the same side of it, with equal vertical angles" have their vertices on one segment of a circle, which Todhunter calls very important, Isaac Todhunter's [*The Elements of Euclid*](https://archive.org/details/todhuntereuclid00todhrich) (1872, free online), his note on III.21 (p. 276), in the notes met under III.3.
- For the test the modern way uses for points on one circle, worked with complex numbers, Titu Andreescu and Dorin Andrica's [*Complex Numbers from A to ... Z*](https://doi.org/10.1007/978-0-8176-8415-0) (2014), §3.2, "Conditions for Collinearity, Orthogonality, and Concyclicity" (pp. 70–73), a problem book that assumes nothing about complex numbers and then turns them on geometry.
