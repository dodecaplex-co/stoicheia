- For De Morgan's remark that III.9 is "a logical equivalent of part of III. 7", which Euclid proves afresh all the same, Augustus De Morgan's ["Short Supplementary Remarks on the First Six Books of Euclid's Elements"](https://archive.org/details/sim_british-almanac-companion_1849) (1849, free online), the remarks on III.9 (p. 10) (also on [III.7](07.md)).
- For the web of Book III's proofs, in which III.9 goes back to the porism of III.1 rather than to III.7, Ian Mueller's [*Philosophy of Mathematics and Deductive Structure in Euclid's Elements*](https://openlibrary.org/books/OL7640284M) (1981), which charts what each proof rests on.
