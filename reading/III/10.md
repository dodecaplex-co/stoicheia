- For the radical axis in the modern way, Kiran S. Kedlaya's [*Geometry Unbound*](https://kskedlaya.org/geometryunbound/) (2006, free online), ch. 6, "Circular reasoning", §6.2, "Radical axis", where the points of equal power with respect to two circles fill a straight line at right angles to the one joining their centres.
- For what an impossible figure can still tell us, Kenneth Manders' ["The Euclidean Diagram (1995)"](https://doi.org/10.1093/acprof:oso/9780199296453.003.0005) (2008) explains why we can read where points lie without trusting an oval to be a circle.
