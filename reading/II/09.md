- Eli Maor's [*The Pythagorean Theorem: A 4,000-Year History*](https://press.princeton.edu/books/paperback/9780691196886/the-pythagorean-theorem) (2007) is a warm life of I.47, the theorem that carries the rest of Book II from here on.
