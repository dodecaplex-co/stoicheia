- What II.8 says about averages, that half the sum of two straight lines is never less than their mean proportional, is the subject of ch. 2, "The AM-GM Inequality", of J. Michael Steele's [*The Cauchy-Schwarz Master Class*](https://doi.org/10.1017/CBO9780511817106) (2004), a friendly course in inequalities.
- More pictures that prove inequalities at a glance, in the spirit of Heath's four equal rectangles set round a square, fill the section "Inequalities" of Roger B. Nelsen's [*Proofs Without Words*](https://doi.org/10.1090/clrm/001) (1993) (also on [II.1](01.md)).
