- For the coordinates of the modern way at their birth, René Descartes' [*Geometry*](https://archive.org/details/geometryofrenede0000davi_t0j4) (1637), in David Eugene Smith and Marcia L. Latham's translation (1925), which turns ruler-and-compass problems into equations, page by page beside the original French (free online).
- For coordinates, vectors and trigonometry at work beside classical proofs, Kiran S. Kedlaya's [*Geometry Unbound*](https://kskedlaya.org/geometryunbound/) (2006), ch. 2 "Algebraic methods", a lively book to read with a pencil, since its PDF has no figures (free online).
