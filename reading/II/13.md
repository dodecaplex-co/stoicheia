- For the coordinates of the modern way at their birth, David Eugene Smith and Marcia L. Latham's translation of [*The Geometry of René Descartes*](https://archive.org/details/geometryofrenede0000davi_t0j4) (1925, free online), Descartes' book of 1637 that turns ruler-and-compass problems into equations, page by page beside the original French.
- For coordinates, vectors and trigonometry at work beside classical proofs, Kiran S. Kedlaya's [*Geometry Unbound*](https://kskedlaya.org/geometryunbound/) (2006, free online), ch. 2, "Algebraic methods", a lively book to read with a pencil, since its PDF has no figures.
