- Why square roots, which this proposition draws, are all that ruler and compass can reach is shown in ch. III, "Geometrical Constructions. The Algebra of Number Fields", of Richard Courant and Herbert Robbins' [*What Is Mathematics?*](https://openlibrary.org/books/OL815900M) (1996).
- No ruler and compass can square the circle, and David S. Richeson's [*Tales of Impossibility*](https://doi.org/10.1515/9780691194233) (2019) tells that story in the chapter "Quadratures".
