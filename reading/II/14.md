- For square roots by ruler and compass, and why they are all that ruler and compass can reach, Richard Courant and Herbert Robbins' [*What Is Mathematics?*](https://openlibrary.org/books/OL815900M) (1941; 2nd ed. 1996), ch. III "Geometrical Constructions. The Algebra of Number Fields", a much-loved classic for curious readers.
- For the one figure no ruler and compass can square, the circle, David S. Richeson's [*Tales of Impossibility*](https://doi.org/10.1515/9780691194233) (2019), the chapter "Quadratures", which picks up where Euclid's squaring of any rectilineal figure leaves off.
