- Thomas L. Heath's [*A History of Greek Mathematics*, vol. 2](https://archive.org/details/cu31924008704227) (1921, free online) gives a chapter each to Heron and Pappus, whom his note here takes for the first exponents of the semi-algebraical method.
- Teachers and editors reworked Euclid's proofs for centuries, as Lardner does in Heath's note here; Benjamin Wardhaugh's [*Encounters with Euclid*](https://press.princeton.edu/books/hardcover/9780691211695/encounters-with-euclid) (2021) tells the story of the *Elements* through such people, who copied, translated, taught and argued over it.
