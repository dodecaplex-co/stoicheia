- For side and diagonal numbers and the equation behind them, d² − 2a² = ±1, John Stillwell's [*Mathematics and Its History*](https://doi.org/10.1007/978-1-4419-6053-5) (2010), the chapter "Greek Number Theory", with its section on Pell's equation.
- For the "take the smaller from the larger" process behind 3/2, 7/5, 17/12 and their ever closer approach to √2, which we now call continued fractions, David Fowler's [*The Mathematics of Plato's Academy*](https://academic.oup.com/book/52809) (1999), ch. 9, "Continued Fractions", beautifully argued.
- For √2 itself, and the machines that have checked the proof that it is irrational, Freek Wiedijk's ["Formalizing 100 Theorems"](https://www.cs.ru.nl/~freek/100/) (free online), no. 1, "The Irrationality of the Square Root of 2", the list met under I.32.
