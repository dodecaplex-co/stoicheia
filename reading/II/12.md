- For the cosine in the modern way, and where trigonometry came from, Eli Maor's [*Trigonometric Delights*](https://press.princeton.edu/books/paperback/9780691202198/trigonometric-delights) (1998), a warm, story-filled history from Egyptian pyramid slopes to imaginary angles.
- For how Greek astronomers turned geometry like Euclid's into tables they could compute with, Glen Van Brummelen's [*The Mathematics of the Heavens and the Earth*](https://press.princeton.edu/books/hardcover/9780691129730/the-mathematics-of-the-heavens-and-the-earth) (2009), the early history of trigonometry told in full.
