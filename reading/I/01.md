- For turning AB through 60° with one multiplication, Tristan Needham's [*Visual Complex Analysis*](https://doi.org/10.1093/oso/9780192868916.001.0001) (2023), ch. 1, "Geometry and Complex Arithmetic", a book that explains complex numbers almost entirely in pictures.
- For the plane of rational points, where the two circles of I.1 slip past each other, Robin Hartshorne's [*Geometry: Euclid and Beyond*](https://doi.org/10.1007/978-0-387-22676-7) (2000), the book the modern way above draws on, written to be read alongside the *Elements*.
- For Euclid in colour, Nicholas Rougeux's [*Byrne's Euclid*](https://www.c82.net/euclid/) (2018, free online), Oliver Byrne's edition of 1847, whose colours our "at a glance" figures borrow, rebuilt for the web with interactive figures.
