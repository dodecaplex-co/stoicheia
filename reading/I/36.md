- For the theory of plane areas behind I.35–I.41, rebuilt with nothing left to the picture, David Hilbert's [*The Foundations of Geometry*](https://www.gutenberg.org/ebooks/17384) (1902, free online), ch. IV, "The Theory of Plane Areas", is short and surprisingly readable.
- For cutting one parallelogram into pieces that rebuild another on an equal base in the same parallels, V. G. Boltyanskii's [*Equivalent and Equidecomposable Figures*](https://store.doverpublications.com/collections/math-algebra/products/9780486170053) (1963) is a slim, gentle book written for readers with only school geometry.
