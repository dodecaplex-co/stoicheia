- For the dot product, which is zero exactly when two directions are at right angles, Grant Sanderson's [*Essence of linear algebra*](https://www.3blue1brown.com/topics/linear-algebra) (2016, free online), ch. 9, "Dot products and duality".
- For how mathematicians came to draw √−1 as a quarter turn, Paul J. Nahin's [*An Imaginary Tale: The Story of √−1*](https://doi.org/10.1515/9781400833894) (1998), told with real enthusiasm.
