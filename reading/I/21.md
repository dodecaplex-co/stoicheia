- For what Euclid's figures may be trusted to show, such as which point lies inside which region, and what they may not, Kenneth Manders' essay ["The Euclidean Diagram"](https://doi.org/10.1093/acprof:oso/9780199296453.003.0005) (written in 1995, published in 2008), on which System E, and so our tacit rules, is built.
- For the angles in a segment of a circle, which Book III will show are all equal, Benno Artmann's [*Euclid: The Creation of Mathematics*](https://doi.org/10.1007/978-1-4612-1412-0) (1999), the chapter "Euclid Book III: About the Circle", a mathematician's affectionate walk through all thirteen books.
