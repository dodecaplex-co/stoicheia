- For more of the complex-number geometry the modern way uses, Liang-shin Hahn's [*Complex Numbers and Geometry*](https://doi.org/10.1090/text/052) (1994), the chapter "Applications to Geometry", quick, clean proofs of classical theorems of the plane.
- For proof by contradiction in Lean, and the classical axioms behind `Classical.byContradiction`, Jeremy Avigad, Leonardo de Moura, Soonho Kong and Sebastian Ullrich's [*Theorem Proving in Lean 4*](https://lean-lang.org/theorem_proving_in_lean4/) (free online), ch. 3, "Propositions and Proofs", and ch. 12, "Axioms and Computation", the official guide to how Lean thinks.
