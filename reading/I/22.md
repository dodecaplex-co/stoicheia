- Richard Courant and Herbert Robbins' [*What Is Mathematics?*](https://openlibrary.org/books/OL815900M) (1996, revised by Ian Stewart), a classic for curious readers since 1941, explains in ch. III, "Geometrical Constructions. The Algebra of Number Fields", why ruler and compass can always reach a square root, such as the height of K above FG.
