- For the twenty centuries of attempts to prove Postulate 5, and the new geometry they led to, Marvin Jay Greenberg's [*Euclidean and Non-Euclidean Geometries*](https://store.macmillanlearning.com/us/product/Euclidean-and-Non-Euclidean-Geometries/p/0716799480) (2008), chs. 5 and 6, "History of the Parallel Postulate" and "The Discovery of Non-Euclidean Geometry", tells the long, very human story through to Bolyai and Lobachevsky.
- For the case Heath reports for keeping Euclid's postulate rather than "Playfair's" axiom, Charles L. Dodgson's [*Euclid and His Modern Rivals*](https://archive.org/details/euclidandhismode000469mbp) (1879, free online) is the book it comes from, a comic dialogue in which Lewis Carroll puts Euclid's rivals on trial.
