- Twenty centuries of attempts to prove Postulate 5, and the new geometry they led to, are the story of chs. 5 and 6 of Marvin Jay Greenberg's [*Euclidean and Non-Euclidean Geometries*](https://store.macmillanlearning.com/us/product/Euclidean-and-Non-Euclidean-Geometries/p/0716799480) (2008), "History of the Parallel Postulate" and "The Discovery of Non-Euclidean Geometry", told through to Bolyai and Lobachevsky in a warm textbook.
- The case Heath reports for keeping Euclid's postulate rather than "Playfair's" axiom is made at length in Charles L. Dodgson's [*Euclid and His Modern Rivals*](https://archive.org/details/euclidandhismode000469mbp) (1879, free online), Lewis Carroll's comic trial of the textbooks of his day (also on [I.7](07.md)).
