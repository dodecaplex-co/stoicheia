- For E as a point a known fraction of the way along AB, Kiran S. Kedlaya's [*Geometry Unbound*](https://kskedlaya.org/geometryunbound/) (2006), ch. 2, "Algebraic methods", a lively book that sets vectors and complex numbers beside classical proofs (free online).
- For what a straightedge and compass can do, now that I.2 and I.3 have given the compass its full power, John Stillwell's [*The Four Pillars of Geometry*](https://doi.org/10.1007/0-387-29052-4) (2005), ch. 1, "Straightedge and compass", a short, clear book that looks at the plane four ways.
