- Grant Sanderson's animated [*Essence of linear algebra*](https://www.3blue1brown.com/topics/linear-algebra) (2016, free online) shows in ch. 6, "The determinant", that a determinant is a signed area, which is why the size of I.37's cross product is twice the area of the triangle ABC (also on [I.2](02.md)).
- A computer can prove geometry theorems by keeping track of the areas of triangles, much as I.35–I.41 do, and Shang-Ching Chou, Xiao-Shan Gao and Jing-Zhong Zhang's [*Machine Proofs in Geometry*](https://doi.org/10.1142/2196) (1994) shows how this area method finds short, readable proofs.
