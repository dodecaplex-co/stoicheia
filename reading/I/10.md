- Why do those rules of "between" matter? In Felix Klein's [*Elementary Mathematics from a Higher Standpoint, Volume II: Geometry*](https://doi.org/10.1007/978-3-662-49445-5) (2016), Gert Schubring's translation of his lectures of 1908, the section on the *Elements* points out that Euclid has no axioms of betweenness, the gap through which the old geometric sophisms slip.
