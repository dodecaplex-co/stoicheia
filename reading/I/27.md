- For the two sides of a straight line, which Euclid reads off his figure and Lean asks for, David Hilbert's [*The Foundations of Geometry*](https://www.gutenberg.org/ebooks/17384) (1902, free online), ch. I, "The Five Groups of Axioms", which writes down the axioms of order behind "between".
- For the directions of the modern way, John Stillwell's [*The Four Pillars of Geometry*](https://doi.org/10.1007/0-387-29052-4) (2005), ch. 4, "Vectors and Euclidean spaces", which carries Euclid's plane over into vectors in one clear chapter.
