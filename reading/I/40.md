- Benjamin Wardhaugh's [*Encounters with Euclid*](https://press.princeton.edu/books/hardcover/9780691211695/encounters-with-euclid) (2021), a charming history of the *Elements* told through the people who copied, translated, taught, drew and argued over it, follows the book through papyri, manuscripts, printed books and classrooms: a long life in which a whole proposition could be added and kept.
- The coordinates of the modern way are set up in ch. 3, "Coordinates", of John Stillwell's [*The Four Pillars of Geometry*](https://doi.org/10.1007/0-387-29052-4) (2005) (also on [I.3](03.md)).
