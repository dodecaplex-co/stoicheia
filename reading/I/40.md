- For the long life of the text, in which a whole proposition could be added and kept, Benjamin Wardhaugh's [*Encounters with Euclid*](https://press.princeton.edu/books/hardcover/9780691211695/encounters-with-euclid) (2021) is a charming history of the *Elements* in papyri, manuscripts, printed books and classrooms.
- For the coordinates of our modern way, John Stillwell's [*The Four Pillars of Geometry*](https://doi.org/10.1007/0-387-29052-4) (2005), ch. 3, "Coordinates", sets Euclid's plane out again in numbers.
