- For sliding BC over to A, parallel to itself, Grant Sanderson's [*Essence of linear algebra*](https://www.3blue1brown.com/topics/linear-algebra) (2016, free online), ch. 1, "Vectors, what even are they?", short animated lessons that make vectors as easy to see as one of Byrne's figures.
- For translations and the other motions of the plane, I. M. Yaglom's [*Geometric Transformations I*](https://doi.org/10.5948/UPO9780883859254) (1962), ch. I, "Displacements", §1, "Translations", a slim, playful book written for curious school students.
