- Glen Van Brummelen's [*The Mathematics of the Heavens and the Earth*](https://press.princeton.edu/books/hardcover/9780691129730/the-mathematics-of-the-heavens-and-the-earth) (2009), a full history of early trigonometry, follows the cosine of the modern way back to the Greek tables built from straight lines joining points on a circle, and lets you watch Greek astronomers turn those straight lines into tables they could compute with.
