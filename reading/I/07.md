- For the moment problems of ruler and compass became equations, as I.7's two circles do in the modern way, David Eugene Smith and Marcia L. Latham's translation of [*The Geometry of René Descartes*](https://archive.org/details/geometryofrenede0000davi_t0j4) (1925), with the French of 1637 in facsimile, page by page (free online).
- For the book Heath quotes on hinged rods, Charles L. Dodgson's [*Euclid and His Modern Rivals*](https://archive.org/details/euclidandhismode000469mbp) (1879), in which Lewis Carroll puts Euclid's modern rivals on trial in a comic dialogue, great fun to read (free online).
