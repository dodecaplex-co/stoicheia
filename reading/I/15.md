- For the half-turn about E, which carries each angle onto the one vertically opposite it, George E. Martin's [*Transformation Geometry*](https://doi.org/10.1007/978-1-4612-5680-9) (1982), ch. 3, "Translations and Halfturns", a clear, step-by-step book on the motions of the plane.
- For Thales, and Eudemus' history of the earliest Greek geometry, Thomas L. Heath's own [*A History of Greek Mathematics*](https://archive.org/details/cu31924008704219) (1921, free online), whose first volume runs from Thales to Euclid.
