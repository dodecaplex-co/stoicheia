- For the reflection that swaps the two equal sides, Pappus' idea in modern dress, I. M. Yaglom's [*Geometric Transformations I*](https://doi.org/10.5948/UPO9780883859254) (1962), ch. II, "Symmetry", §1, "Reflection and Glide Reflection", with reflections put to work on construction problems.
- For the long life of the *Elements* in classrooms, where generations of schoolchildren met the bridge of asses, Benjamin Wardhaugh's [*Encounters with Euclid*](https://press.princeton.edu/books/hardcover/9780691211695/encounters-with-euclid) (2021), a charming history of the people who copied, translated, taught and argued over Euclid's book.
