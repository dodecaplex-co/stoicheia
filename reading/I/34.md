- I. M. Yaglom's [*Geometric Transformations I*](https://doi.org/10.5948/UPO9780883859254) (1962), a slim, playful classic in Allen Shields' translation, puts the half-turn of the modern way to work in ch. I, §2, "Half Turn and Rotation".
- Why one motion that keeps every length, angle and area says so much is the theme of Felix Klein's Erlangen Programme, ["A comparative review of recent researches in geometry"](https://arxiv.org/abs/0807.3161) (1893, free online), a short manifesto (also on [I.8](08.md)).
