- For the half-turn of the modern way, I. M. Yaglom's [*Geometric Transformations I*](https://doi.org/10.5948/UPO9780883859254) (1962), ch. I, §2, "Half Turn and Rotation", in Allen Shields' translation, a slim, playful classic that puts each motion to work on clever constructions.
- For why one motion that keeps every length, angle and area says so much, Felix Klein's ["A comparative review of recent researches in geometry"](https://arxiv.org/abs/0807.3161) (1893, free online), his Erlangen Programme of 1872 in M. W. Haskell's translation, the short manifesto behind the idea that a geometry is defined by the motions it allows.
