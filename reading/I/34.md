- For the half-turn of our modern way, I. M. Yaglom's [*Geometric Transformations I*](https://doi.org/10.5948/UPO9780883859254) (1962), ch. I, §2, "Half Turn and Rotation", in Allen Shields' translation, is a slim, playful classic that puts each motion to work on clever constructions.
- For why one motion that keeps every length, angle and area says so much, Felix Klein's [*A Comparative Review of Recent Researches in Geometry*](https://arxiv.org/abs/0807.3161) (1893, free online), his Erlangen Programme of 1872 in M. W. Haskell's translation, is the short manifesto behind the idea that a geometry is defined by the motions it allows.
