- For arguments that add when complex numbers multiply, and for the geometry of the sphere, where I.16 fails, Tristan Needham's [*Visual Complex Analysis*](https://doi.org/10.1093/oso/9780192868916.001.0001) (2023), ch. 1, "Geometry and Complex Arithmetic", and ch. 6, "Non-Euclidean Geometry", which draws the sphere as vividly as the plane.
- For the geometry that stands without the parallel postulate, where I.16 is known as the exterior angle theorem, Marvin Jay Greenberg's [*Euclidean and Non-Euclidean Geometries*](https://store.macmillanlearning.com/us/product/Euclidean-and-Non-Euclidean-Geometries/p/0716799480) (2008), ch. 4, "Neutral Geometry", which shows what Euclid can prove without the fifth postulate.
