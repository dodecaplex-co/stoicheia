- For the figure that no ruler and compass can turn into a square, the circle, Felix Klein's [*Famous Problems of Elementary Geometry*](https://archive.org/details/famousproblemsof00kleiiala) (1897, free online), in Wooster Woodruff Beman and David Eugene Smith's translation, shows why in about eighty friendly pages.
- For whether applying an area really is division, as our modern way says, Jens Høyrup's [*What is 'geometric algebra', and what has it been in historiography?*](https://doi.org/10.3934/Math.2017.1.128) (2017, free online) is a guided tour of fifty years of argument, and a gentle way into it.
