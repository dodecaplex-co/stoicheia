- For the whole story of the parallel postulate, which "Playfair's" axiom is one more way of saying, Roberto Bonola's [*Non-Euclidean Geometry*](https://archive.org/details/noneuclideangeom00bonorich) (1912, free online), in H. S. Carslaw's translation, is a classic history from the old commentators through Saccheri to Bolyai and Lobachevsky.
- For straight lines given by a point and a direction, as in our modern way, Kiran S. Kedlaya's [*Geometry Unbound*](https://kskedlaya.org/geometryunbound/) (2006, free online), ch. 2, "Algebraic methods", sets vectors and coordinates beside classical proofs.
