- Roberto Bonola's [*Non-Euclidean Geometry*](https://archive.org/details/noneuclideangeom00bonorich) (1912, free online), in H. S. Carslaw's translation, tells the long story of the parallel postulate, from the old commentators, Proclus among them, through Saccheri to Bolyai and Lobachevsky, and tells it like someone who loved it.
- The geometry of János Bolyai and Nikolai Lobachevsky, where I.17 still holds, is the last stop of John Stillwell's [*The Four Pillars of Geometry*](https://doi.org/10.1007/0-387-29052-4) (2005), ch. 8, "Non-Euclidean geometry" (also on [I.3](03.md)).
