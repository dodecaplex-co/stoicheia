- For the long story of the parallel postulate, from the old commentators, Proclus among them, through Saccheri to Bolyai and Lobachevsky, Roberto Bonola's [*Non-Euclidean Geometry*](https://archive.org/details/noneuclideangeom00bonorich) (1912, free online), in H. S. Carslaw's translation, told by someone who clearly loved the story.
- For the geometry of János Bolyai and Nikolai Lobachevsky, where I.17 still holds, John Stillwell's [*The Four Pillars of Geometry*](https://doi.org/10.1007/0-387-29052-4) (2005), ch. 8, "Non-Euclidean geometry", the book's last stop.
