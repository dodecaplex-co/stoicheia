- For the coordinates of the modern way, where a triangle's height becomes the number y, David Eugene Smith and Marcia L. Latham's translation of [*The Geometry of René Descartes*](https://archive.org/details/geometryofrenede0000davi_t0j4) (1925, free online), the book met under I.7, where coordinates began.
- For Euclid's "if not" and "which is impossible", Daniel J. Velleman's [*How To Prove It with Lean*](https://djvelleman.github.io/HTPIwL/) (free online), the section "Proofs Involving Negations and Conditionals", in the book met under I.19, where proof by contradiction is the tactic `by_contra`.
