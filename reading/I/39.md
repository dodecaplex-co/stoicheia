- For the coordinates of our modern way, where a triangle's height becomes the number y, David Eugene Smith and Marcia L. Latham's [*The Geometry of René Descartes*](https://archive.org/details/geometryofrenede0000davi_t0j4) (1925, free online) is where coordinates began: Descartes' book of 1637 in English, page by page beside the French.
- For Euclid's "if not" and "which is impossible", Daniel J. Velleman's [*How To Prove It with Lean*](https://djvelleman.github.io/HTPIwL/) (free online), the section "Proofs Involving Negations and Conditionals", shows proof by contradiction done in Lean, where it is the tactic `by_contra`.
