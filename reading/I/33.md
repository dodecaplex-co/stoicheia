- B − A = D − C says that one motion, a translation, carries AB onto CD, and George E. Martin's clear, step-by-step [*Transformation Geometry*](https://doi.org/10.1007/978-1-4612-5680-9) (1982) studies such motions in ch. 3, "Translations and Halfturns" (also on [I.15](15.md)).
- What each part of Book I is for, the parallels and the parallelograms among them, is the theme of the chapter "Euclid Book I: Basic Geometry" in Benno Artmann's [*Euclid: The Creation of Mathematics*](https://doi.org/10.1007/978-1-4612-1412-0) (1999), a mathematician's walk through all thirteen books.
