- For the cosine in the modern way, and how trigonometry grew out of Greek tables of chords, Eli Maor's [*Trigonometric Delights*](https://press.princeton.edu/books/paperback/9780691202198/trigonometric-delights) (1998), ch. 2, "Chords", a warm, story-filled history.
- For arguing "by division", Daniel J. Velleman's [*How to Prove It*](https://doi.org/10.1017/9781108539890) (2019), ch. 3, "Proofs", which takes proof by cases and proof by contradiction one gentle step at a time.
