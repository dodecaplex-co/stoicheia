- Eli Maor's [*Trigonometric Delights*](https://press.princeton.edu/books/paperback/9780691202198/trigonometric-delights) (1998), a story-filled history of trigonometry, traces the cosine of the modern way back to the Greek tables of chords in ch. 2, "Chords".
- Arguing "by division" is proof by cases, and Daniel J. Velleman's [*How to Prove It*](https://doi.org/10.1017/9781108539890) (2019), a patient introduction to the logic under every proof, takes it one step at a time in ch. 3, "Proofs".
