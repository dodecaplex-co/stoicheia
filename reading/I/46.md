- For the turn through a right angle that multiplying by i makes, Paul J. Nahin's [*An Imaginary Tale*](https://doi.org/10.1515/9781400833894) (1998) tells, with real enthusiasm, how mathematicians went from distrusting √−1 to drawing it as a quarter turn.
- For more of Euclid's geometry done with complex numbers, Liang-shin Hahn's [*Complex Numbers and Geometry*](https://doi.org/10.1090/text/052) (1994) assumes nothing about them and then uses them for quick, satisfying proofs of classic theorems.
