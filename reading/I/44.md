- Thomas L. Heath's own [*A History of Greek Mathematics*](https://archive.org/details/cu31924008704219) (1921, free online) follows the application of areas back past the *Elements* to the Pythagoreans, in ch. V, "Pythagorean Geometry", of its first volume, *From Thales to Euclid* (also on [I.15](15.md)).
- Jens Høyrup's [*Algebra in Cuneiform*](https://www.mprl-series.mpg.de/textbooks/2/) (2017, free online) is a readable introduction to the Babylonian way of solving problems with squares and rectangles, a cut-and-paste cousin of applying an area; begin with ch. 3, "The Fundamental Techniques for the Second Degree".
