- For the story of applying areas before and after Euclid, Thomas L. Heath's [*A History of Greek Mathematics*](https://archive.org/details/cu31924008704219) (1921, free online) is his own big history, the place to follow a method back past the *Elements*.
- For the Babylonian way of solving problems with squares and rectangles, a cut-and-paste cousin of applying an area, Jens Høyrup's [*Algebra in Cuneiform*](https://www.mprl-series.mpg.de/textbooks/2/) (2017, free online), ch. 3, "The Fundamental Techniques for the Second Degree", is a readable introduction.
