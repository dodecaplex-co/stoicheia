- David Hilbert's [*The Foundations of Geometry*](https://www.gutenberg.org/ebooks/17384) (1902, free online), in E. J. Townsend's translation, is the short, surprisingly readable book that writes down the axioms Euclid took for granted; ch. I, "The Five Groups of Axioms", makes side, angle, side one of them.
- To see superposition as a motion of the plane, read Felix Klein's Erlangen Programme of 1872, ["A comparative review of recent researches in geometry"](https://arxiv.org/abs/0807.3161) (1893, free online), in M. W. Haskell's translation: the short manifesto behind the idea that a geometry is defined by the motions it allows.
