- For side–angle–side made an axiom, David Hilbert's [*The Foundations of Geometry*](https://www.gutenberg.org/ebooks/17384) (1902, free online), ch. I, "The Five Groups of Axioms", in E. J. Townsend's translation, the short, surprisingly readable book that writes down the axioms Euclid took for granted.
- For superposition as a motion of the plane, Felix Klein's Erlangen Programme of 1872, ["A comparative review of recent researches in geometry"](https://arxiv.org/abs/0807.3161) (1893, free online), in M. W. Haskell's translation, the short manifesto behind the idea that a geometry is defined by the motions it allows.
