- For the cross products of the modern way grown into one formula for any polygon, Bart Braden's ["The Surveyor's Area Formula"](https://web.archive.org/web/20140629065751/https://www.maa.org/sites/default/files/pdf/pubs/Calc_Articles/ma063.pdf) (1986, free online), a friendly article on the "shoelace" formula: give it the corners of a field and it hands back the area.
- For more cutting and rebuilding like Proclus' trapezium, which becomes a triangle when one triangle is moved, Greg N. Frederickson's [*Dissections: Plane & Fancy*](https://www.cs.purdue.edu/homes/gnf/book.html) (1997), a gorgeous picture book of figures cut into pieces that rebuild other figures.
