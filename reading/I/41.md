- Bart Braden's ["The Surveyor's Area Formula"](https://web.archive.org/web/20140629065751/https://www.maa.org/sites/default/files/pdf/pubs/Calc_Articles/ma063.pdf) (1986, free online) is a friendly article on the "shoelace" formula, which takes the corners of a field and hands back its area. The formula cuts the field into triangles from one point and takes each as half of a parallelogram, the relation I.41 proves, worked out in coordinates.
- Proclus' trapezium becomes a triangle when one small triangle is moved, and Greg N. Frederickson's [*Dissections: Plane & Fancy*](https://www.cs.purdue.edu/homes/gnf/book.html) (1997) is a gorgeous picture book of such cutting and rebuilding.
