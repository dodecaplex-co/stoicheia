- Tristan Needham's [*Visual Complex Analysis*](https://doi.org/10.1093/oso/9780192868916.001.0001) (2023), complex numbers almost entirely in pictures, explains angles as arguments and multiplication as turning and stretching, the geometry behind the modern way, in ch. 1, "Geometry and Complex Arithmetic" (also on [I.1](01.md)).
- Freek Wiedijk's ["Formalizing 100 Theorems"](https://www.cs.ru.nl/~freek/100/) (free online) lists I.32 as no. 27, "Sum of the Angles of a Triangle", among the famous theorems checked by machine, and names the systems that have checked it.
