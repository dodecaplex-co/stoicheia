- George E. Martin's [*Transformation Geometry*](https://doi.org/10.1007/978-1-4612-5680-9) (1982), a clear, step-by-step book, builds congruence itself out of reflections, and its ch. 5, "Congruence", shows superposition as a rigid motion.
- Where did the sine and cosine of the modern way come from? Eli Maor's [*Trigonometric Delights*](https://press.princeton.edu/books/paperback/9780691202198/trigonometric-delights) (1998) tells it as a warm, story-filled history, from Egyptian pyramid slopes and Greek tables built from straight lines joining points on a circle to imaginary angles.
