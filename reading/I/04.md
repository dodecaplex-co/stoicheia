- For superposition as a rigid motion, George E. Martin's [*Transformation Geometry*](https://doi.org/10.1007/978-1-4612-5680-9) (1982), ch. 5, "Congruence", a clear, step-by-step book that builds congruence itself out of reflections.
- For where the sine and cosine of the modern way came from, Eli Maor's [*Trigonometric Delights*](https://press.princeton.edu/books/paperback/9780691202198/trigonometric-delights) (1998), a warm, story-filled history of trigonometry, from Egyptian pyramid slopes and Greek tables of chords to imaginary angles.
