- George E. Martin's textbook [*Transformation Geometry*](https://doi.org/10.1007/978-1-4612-5680-9) (1982) builds congruence itself out of reflections, and its ch. 5, "Congruence", shows superposition as a rigid motion.
- Eli Maor's [*Trigonometric Delights*](https://press.princeton.edu/books/paperback/9780691202198/trigonometric-delights) (1998), a history of trigonometry told in stories, follows the sine and cosine of the modern way from Egyptian pyramid slopes, through Greek tables built from straight lines in a circle, to imaginary angles.
