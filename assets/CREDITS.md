# Credits

The Stoicheia logo is built on a detail of a painting that is more than five hundred years old. We're grateful to everyone who made it, kept it, and photographed it.

## The files

- `stoicheia-social.png` (1280 × 640): the social preview, also shown at the top of the README.
- `stoicheia-logo.png` (1024 × 1024), `stoicheia-logo-512.png` and `stoicheia-logo-128.png`: the logo cropped square, at three sizes, for an avatar or an icon. Nothing in the repository uses them yet; they are here for anyone who wants the logo.
- `i47-grid.svg`: the figure of I.47 on the laptop's screen.

## The painting

With gratitude to **Raphael** (Raffaello Sanzio da Urbino, 1483–1520), who painted ***The School of Athens*** (*Scuola di Atene*), a fresco made in 1509–1511 for the Stanza della Segnatura in the Apostolic Palace, Vatican City. It is cared for today by the **Vatican Museums** (Musei Vaticani).

The logo uses the group in the lower right of the fresco: a bald geometer, usually identified as **Euclid** (sometimes as Archimedes), bends over with a pair of compasses while his students watch. In our version, his slate has turned into a laptop and he is working through *Elements* I.47.

## What we changed

The slate on the floor was digitally replaced by an open laptop, set into the fresco's perspective. Its screen shows *Elements* I.47 as a 3-4-5 right triangle with the squares on its sides (9 + 16 = 25 cells) drawn on a grid. Euclid's hand and compasses were brought forward so that he is drawing on the screen. Everything that isn't Raphael's (the laptop, the screen figure in `i47-grid.svg`, and the social-preview layout) was made for the Stoicheia project. The text in the social preview is set in Baskerville.
